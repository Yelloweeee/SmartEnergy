Your appliances shouldn’t run the moment you press start — they should wait for clean power.

Grid Pulse watches real-time carbon intensity on the electricity grid and switches your washing machine, dishwasher and EV charger on automatically when the energy mix is at its cleanest. Same cycle, same runtime — far fewer emissions, with nothing for you to do.

01The problem

Grid carbon intensity moves all day. Your appliances ignore it.

Almost every device in a home runs on demand, regardless of how dirty the grid is at that second. But the mix shifts constantly — cleaner around midday when solar peaks, much dirtier through the evening ramp when gas plants come online.

Most people have zero visibility into any of it, so the choice never gets made.

02How it works

Four steps, running quietly in the background.

  1. 01

    Read the grid

    Pulls live carbon intensity data for your region and tracks how clean the electricity mix is, hour by hour.

  2. 02

    Find the clean window

    For each appliance, it scans the allowed time window and picks the single hour with the lowest carbon intensity.

  3. 03

    Wait, then connect

    The appliance stays idle until that hour arrives, then plugs itself in and starts automatically.

  4. 04

    Show the impact

    Calculates the CO2 avoided by waiting, compared against running the moment you pressed start.
